- Morning Brief. With AI summaries installed and enabled, the server pre-generates a short daily brief (a narrative paragraph plus bullet highlights) around 05:00 server-local from yesterday's captures, today's due To Dos and pinned items. It is cached per day, regenerated only on explicit request, and served at GET /v1/morning-brief. The morning review renders it as read-only markdown at the top of the screen.
