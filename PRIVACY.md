# Tangent Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 9, 2026

Tangent ("the app") is a local-first voice note, to-do, and notebook app published
by GyratingSeaCow (dev.tangent.tangent).

## The short version

Tangent does not collect your data. Period.

## What the app stores

- Voice recordings, transcripts, notes, to-dos, ink/notebook pages, and PDF
  imports are stored **locally on your device**.
- If you choose to configure a sync server, your data syncs **only to the server
  you yourself host and control** (the Tangent server component from this
  repository). No Tangent data ever passes through servers operated by the
  developer, because none exist.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you configure it:

- **Optional self-hosted sync/transcription server:** data goes only to the
  server address you enter, over your own network or a connection you control.
- There are no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporters, no advertising
  SDKs, and no third-party data sharing of any kind in the app.

## Permissions the app requests and why

- **Microphone** — to record voice notes. Requested only when you record.
- **Notifications** — to remind you about to-dos with due times and daily
  reviews. Requested only when you first set one up.
- **Exact alarms (Android 12+)** — so due-time reminders fire at the exact
  minute you chose. Requested only from the reminder features.

All permissions are optional; the app works without them with reduced features.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data lives in the app's local storage (and on your own server if you set
one up). Deleting items in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling
the app removes the data. The developer cannot access, retain, or delete your
data because the developer never receives it.

## Children

Tangent does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children, because it collects no personal information at all.
